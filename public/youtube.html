How it works

Each character has its own debug Chrome (unique port, logged into that channel) and its own inbox folder. First time: launch the character's Chrome with the command on its card and log into its channel once — the per-port profile keeps that login. After that: tick already logged in and just hit Schedule — it auto-launches that character's Chrome (if not already open) and opens studio.youtube.com for you. Videos are scheduled at fixed viral-US times, 3/day by default, starting today, packing around whatever's already scheduled — and removed from the folder once scheduled.
